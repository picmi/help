# Employment agreements and your information

When you accept an employment agreement in PICMI, the agreement is completed with the personal details and employment
information provided during your application. PICMI creates a record for each party: one for you and one for the
employer. Each party can access their record by signing in to their own PICMI account.

## Access your agreement

You can view your application and employment agreement in PICMI after signing in with the email address you used for
your application. See [Signing in](sign-in.md) for help accessing your account.

The employer can access its record through its own PICMI account. Your record and the employer's record are available
to each party through their respective logins.

## Employer copies and external systems

An employer may download or otherwise copy information available to it in PICMI. Once the employer has a separate copy,
how it stores, uses, or shares that copy is governed by the employer's own privacy practices and obligations.

PICMI also provides employers with ways to transfer application information to payroll and other systems, either
manually or through configured integrations. Whether information is sent to an external system depends on the
employer's use of those options. PICMI cannot confirm from this general article whether a particular employer has
downloaded or transferred your information.

For questions about information held by an employer outside PICMI, contact the employer directly. For help accessing
your application or agreement in PICMI, see [How do I get access to my employment agreement and job details?](../faqs.md#platform-usage).
