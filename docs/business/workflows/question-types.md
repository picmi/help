# Choose the right question type

Choose the type that matches the answer you need to collect. Using a specific type helps participants answer clearly
and makes the information easier to review later.

| Question type       | Description                              | Example                                        |
|---------------------|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| **Text**            | Free-text answer.                        | “Tell us about your previous experience.”      |
| **Address**         | Physical or street address.              | “What is the worksite address?”                |
| **Phone**           | Phone number.                            | “What is the best number to reach you on?”     |
| **Email**           | Email address.                           | “What is your email address?”                  |
| **Number**          | Numeric answer.                          | “How many years have you worked in this role?” |
| **NZ bank account** | New Zealand bank account number.         | “What is your bank account number?”            |
| **AU bank account** | Australian bank account number.          | “What is your bank account number?”            |
| **AU BSB**          | Australian BSB or bank-routing number.   | “What is your BSB?”                            |
| **NZ IRD**          | New Zealand IRD number.                  | “What is your IRD number?”                     |
| **AU TFN**          | Australian tax file number.              | “What is your TFN?”                            |
| **File**            | File upload.                             | “Upload your passport.”                        |
| **Date**            | Calendar date.                           | “What date can you start?”                     |
| **Single select**   | One option from a list.                  | “Which role are you applying for?”             |
| **Multi-select**    | One or more options from a list.         | “Which sites can you work at?”                 |
| **Declaration**     | Yes with a description, or no.           | “Do you have anything to declare?”             |
| **Accept**          | An either-or acceptance or confirmation. | “Do you agree to these terms?”                 |
| **Table**           | Structured tabular data.                 | “List your subcontractors.”                    |

## Decision guide

- Use **Text** for words or sentences that do not need a specialised format.
- Use **Number** for quantities, hours, rates, or counts.
- Use **Single select** when only one choice should be selected.
- Use **Accept** for a direct acceptance or confirmation.
- Use **Multi-select** when participants can choose more than one option.
- Use **Declaration** when a “yes” answer must include an explanation.
- Use **File** for evidence or documents.
- Use **Table** when you need consistent rows and columns for later review.

## Example

For a contractor onboarding workflow, use **Accept** for “Do you agree to the public liability insurance requirement?”,
**File** for the
insurance certificate, and **Table** for the list of subcontractors. This is clearer than asking for all three answers
in
one Text question.
